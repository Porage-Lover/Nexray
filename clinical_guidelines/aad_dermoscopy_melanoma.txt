AAD DERMOSCOPY GUIDELINES FOR MELANOMA

# Overview
The American Academy of Dermatology (AAD) guidelines for the use of dermoscopy focus on distinguishing benign pigmented lesions from malignant melanoma. Dermoscopy improves diagnostic accuracy compared to naked-eye examination.

# The ABCDE Rule
Clinical warning signs for melanoma:
- **A - Asymmetry:** One half of the lesion does not match the other.
- **B - Border Irregularity:** Edges are ragged, notched, or blurred.
- **C - Color Variation:** Pigmentation is not uniform. Shades of brown, black, red, white, or blue may be present.
- **D - Diameter:** Greater than 6mm (though some melanomas are diagnosed at smaller sizes).
- **E - Evolving:** The lesion is changing in size, shape, color, or elevating over time.

# Specific Dermoscopic Structures
- **Atypical Pigment Networks:** Irregular meshes with thickened, darkened lines and variable spaces. Highly indicative of melanoma.
- **Blue-White Veil:** Irregular, structureless areas of confluent blue pigmentation with an overlying white "ground-glass" haze. Strongly associated with invasive melanoma.
- **Atypical Vascular Patterns:** Polymorphous vessels (e.g., linear irregular, dotted, or hairpin vessels) scattered asymmetrically. Common in hypopigmented or amelanotic melanoma.
- **Irregular Streaks:** Linear pigmentations at the periphery of a lesion extending radially (pseudopods or radial streaming), indicating asymmetrical growth.
- **Irregular Dots/Globules:** Asymmetrically distributed black or brown dots/globules, particularly at the periphery.

# Common Benign Findings
- **Typical Pigment Network:** Symmetrical, fading at the periphery.
- **Comedo-like Openings & Milia-like Cysts:** Classic features of seborrheic keratoses.
- **Homogeneous Blue Pattern:** Typically seen in benign blue nevi.

# Recommended Actions
- **Benign Pattern Identified:** No excision needed. Reassure the patient and return to routine skin checks.
- **Equivocal Lesion (Mild Atypia):** Consider short-term digital dermoscopy follow-up (3-6 months) to monitor for change.
- **Suspicious for Melanoma (Significant Atypia / ABCDE criteria met):** Complete excisional biopsy with 1-3mm margins is strongly recommended for histopathological confirmation. DO NOT perform superficial shave biopsies on highly suspicious lesions.
