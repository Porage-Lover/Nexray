SNMMI PET/CT ONCOLOGY GUIDELINES

# Overview
The Society of Nuclear Medicine and Molecular Imaging (SNMMI) provides criteria for the use of 18F-FDG PET/CT in oncology. It evaluates the altered glucose metabolism of tumors for staging, restaging, and assessing treatment response.

# SUVmax Interpretation
- **SUV (Standardized Uptake Value):** A semi-quantitative measure of radiotracer concentration. 
- **SUVmax:** The single hottest pixel within a region of interest. It is highly dependent on patient body weight, injected dose, and uptake time.
- **Pathologic Uptake:** Malignant cells generally exhibit increased glycolysis. An SUVmax significantly higher than surrounding background tissue (often >2.5 to 3.0, though highly variable depending on tumor type) is suspicious for malignancy.
- **Physiologic Uptake:** Brain, myocardium, urinary tract (kidneys, ureters, bladder), liver, and vocal cords normally concentrate FDG. Brown fat activation can cause intense, symmetric neck and supraclavicular uptake.

# Determining Metabolic Response to Therapy
PET/CT is crucial for assessing response to chemotherapy and immunotherapy, notably in lymphomas and solid tumors.

## Deauville Criteria (Lymphoma)
Uses a 5-point scale comparing residual tumor uptake to normal reference organs:
- **Score 1:** No uptake above background.
- **Score 2:** Uptake ≤ mediastinal blood pool (MBP).
- **Score 3:** Uptake > MBP but ≤ liver.
- **Score 4:** Uptake moderately > liver.
- **Score 5:** Uptake markedly higher than liver and/or new lesions.

**Interpretation:**
- Scores 1, 2, and 3: Complete metabolic response (CMR).
- Scores 4 and 5: Treatment failure, residual disease, or progression.

# Recommended Actions
- **Suspicious Isolated Uptake:** Correlation with diagnostic CT or MRI. Consider image-guided biopsy to confirm malignancy.
- **Physiologic Uptake / Artifacts:** Reassure, utilize warming protocols to reduce brown fat uptake in future scans.
- **Complete Metabolic Response (Deauville 1-3):** Continue routine clinical and imaging surveillance.
- **Progressive Disease / High Residual Uptake (Deauville 4-5):** Alter systemic therapy regimen, consider radiation therapy or salvage surgery. Evaluate for biopsy to confirm refractory disease versus inflammatory reaction.
