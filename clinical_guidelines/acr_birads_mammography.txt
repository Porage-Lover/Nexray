ACR BI-RADS MAMMOGRAPHY GUIDELINES

# Overview
The American College of Radiology (ACR) Breast Imaging-Reporting and Data System (BI-RADS) is a risk assessment and quality assurance tool in mammography, ultrasound, and MRI.

# BI-RADS Categories
- **BI-RADS 0:** Incomplete. Need additional imaging evaluation (e.g., spot compression, magnification, or ultrasound) or prior mammograms for comparison.
- **BI-RADS 1:** Negative. Symmetrical and no masses, architectural distortion, or suspicious calcifications. Routine screening.
- **BI-RADS 2:** Benign. 0% probability of malignancy. Includes calcified fibroadenomas, multiple secretory calcifications, fat-containing lesions. Routine screening.
- **BI-RADS 3:** Probably Benign. <2% probability of malignancy. Non-palpable, circumscribed mass on a baseline mammogram, focal asymmetry, or cluster of punctate calcifications. Recommended short-interval follow-up (6 months).
- **BI-RADS 4:** Suspicious. 2% to 94% probability of malignancy. No classic appearance of malignancy but sufficiently suspicious to justify a biopsy.
  - 4A: Low suspicion (2% to 10%)
  - 4B: Moderate suspicion (11% to 50%)
  - 4C: High suspicion (51% to 94%)
- **BI-RADS 5:** Highly Suggestive of Malignancy. ≥95% probability of malignancy. Classic signs like spiculated, irregular high-density masses, fine linear or branching calcifications. Tissue diagnosis required.
- **BI-RADS 6:** Known Biopsy-Proven Malignancy. Used for imaging studies acquired prior to surgical excision.

# Key Findings and Morphology
- **Microcalcifications:** Suspicious morphologies include amorphous, coarse heterogeneous, fine pleomorphic, and fine linear or fine linear branching.
- **Mass Margins:** Spiculated, indistinct, or microlobulated margins carry a higher risk of malignancy compared to circumscribed margins.
- **Architectural Distortion:** Disruption of normal breast architecture without a visible mass. Highly suspicious, often warranting biopsy or further evaluation.

# Recommended Actions
- **Category 0:** Recall patient for additional imaging.
- **Category 1 & 2:** Return to routine screening protocols (typically annual).
- **Category 3:** Short-interval follow-up, typically unilateral mammography at 6 months.
- **Category 4 & 5:** Tissue diagnosis is recommended (e.g., ultrasound-guided core biopsy, stereotactic biopsy).
- **Category 6:** Proceed with definitive surgical or oncological management.
